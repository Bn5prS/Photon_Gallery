# Release Notes — v4.2.1

### New Features & Improvements
* **Video Controls Quick Sheet**: Adjust playback speed (0.25×–2.0×), rotate 90°, toggle loop playback, and switch between Fit / Fill aspect modes directly from the video player.
* **Refined Mosaic Grid Layout**: Date-grouped hero images now anchor consistently to the left/start, keeping natural reading order and swipe navigation intuitive.

---

### Bug Fixes
* Fixed external image launch where photos failed to remain open or crashed when opened from third-party apps (#49).
* Fixed navigation swipe confusion across date groups in mosaic layout.

---
